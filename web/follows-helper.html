Twitch Live to Discord

Export the streamers you follow

This helper builds a bookmarklet that reads your own Twitch following page in your existing browser session. No Twitch sign-in, no app registration, and no data leaves your browser.

  1. Drag the purple button to your bookmarks bar. If the bar is hidden, show it first (usually Ctrl/Cmd + Shift + B).
  2. Open twitch.tv/directory/following/channels and wait for the channel cards to appear.
  3. Click the bookmark. It scrolls the page, collects your channel links, and shows an editable list. Copy it, then paste it into the notifier's import dialog.

Manual copy / console fallback

Some browsers block dragging bookmarklets, and some pages block javascript: URLs through their security policy. If clicking the bookmark does nothing, open DevTools (F12) → Console on the Twitch following page, paste the console script below, and press Enter. It runs in the page's existing session and only reads rendered links.

The bookmarklet only reads links already rendered in your browser, displays them in an overlay, and copies them to your clipboard. It does not contact this app, Twitch's API, Discord, or any other server.
